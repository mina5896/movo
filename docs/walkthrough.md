# Walkthrough script (about 5 minutes)

A suggested order for recording a demo. Start from `npm run dev` with a seeded database.

1. **The parent's side (1 min).** Open `/`, enter a first name, and ask "do u have anything sat morning for 2 kids, 5 and 7". Point out: correct programs, the 9:00 class being full, the sibling discount, no pressure. Then click 📞 and say "can someone just call me". It continues the same conversation by voice and asks for a number.
2. **A hard case (30 s).** Text "Coach Mike yelled at my daughter at practice". It's warm, doesn't take sides, and hands off.
3. **The owner's inbox (30 s).** Open `/owner`. The coach conversation is at the top with the reason. Open it, show the text and call turns in one thread, reply as the owner, and show the reply appear in the parent chat.
4. **Correcting it (1 min).** Under an assistant reply, click *Something wrong with this reply?* and write a plain note. Show the proposed change and the new test, then approve.
5. **Checking a change before parents see it (1.5 min).** Open *Club details & rules* and change the summer camp dates. Use *Try your changes* to ask about camp. Click *Check how this changes answers* and show the before/after page: what got better, what (if anything) got worse, side by side. Then publish.
6. **Trusting the scores (30 s).** Open *How it's doing*: the score per category and the list of tests, including the ones that came from corrections. Open *Spot-check the grader* and label one answer.

Close on the main point: every change is tried, checked against every past mistake, and only then published.
